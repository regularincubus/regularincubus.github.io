Chapter Nineteen

it has been a week since you lost yourselves in pleasure for the first time. a week ever since you took her virginity, and the thought still made your mind spin and your body burn nonetheless. to think that something so intimate, such grace, had been bestowed upon you, someone so seemingly irrelevant - it couldn’t quite get into your head, yet you were not complaining, either.

if anything, you took pride in how it went. the clumsy nature of your first encounter was more than welcomed, especially for someone like her. you were sure that people expected something different from her, yet in the end, she was just a woman grinding against you like no other, letting herself indulge in something sinfully delicious just once. maybe twice. maybe more, in the end.

else you wouldn’t find yourself next to her right now, your head on her shoulder as you took a break from the sweat breaking training, simply watching the forest breathing, existing, while enjoying the proximity you shared. your pinky brushed hers, intertwining softly as you looked down on your linked hands.

“is something the matter?” she asked you quietly, gazing at one of the anthills in front of you. she had never been so serene before, a nice change for sure.

“i cannot help but notice you had been rather quiet ever since our, well, our encounter recently.”

you smiled softly, leaning further against her. closing your eyes in the process, you felt the relaxation washing over you, and the faint soreness in your back leaving.

“‘was just thinking of that, too. like, how i was your first and so on.” you said, a slight chuckle following.

she stayed silent at that, and you knew she was thinking about what to respond. you couldn’t help but wonder what’s going on in her head, and the thought amused you; the rising feeling of expecting a change of topic occurred to you, and you smiled to yourself.

“it appears you take great pride in that fact.” she said, her gaze still not meeting yours. she looked down at your hands, her eyes tracing the outlines of your fingers. you could see it, how they worked, following every contour of pale and blackened hands intertwining. it was adorable of sorts, that you partly became a way of distracting her.

“it’s not everyday that that happens, you know.” you responded. “and what can i say? the thought of me being the only one does awaken some rather special feelings, to say the least.”

“special as in possessive?” she asked with a slight smirk.

“special as in, someone like you, who carried herself with such strength, such grace and regality - you were able to be so painfully human with me.” you corrected her, but she didn’t let go.

she averted her gaze from the anthill, looking at you. her eyes were soft, wide; unknown territory.

“that excites you, i presume?” she asked with a raised brow. despite her desperate attempt at seeming nonchalant, you quickly realize just how soft she had gotten over the weeks. there was no secrecy, no mischief, and for the first time you felt like you could actually see through her.

“it intrigues me.” you replied simply. 

her finger held yours tightly, and you felt the warmth radiating from her. for some reason, this felt more intimate than most things you did; as if it weren't arlecchino the knave, number four of the fatui harbingers and you, vanessa, a mere barkeeper from mondstadt.

“how so?” came another question

“didn’t i just explain that?” you threw back.

it wasn’t hard to understand at the end of the day; but there was this nagging feeling deep down that told you the knave was feeling unworthy herself. and perhaps that was the very root of it all - an inability to understand just how deprived of want, genuine want, and connection she must have been.

truth be told, it wasn’t much of a surprise. from what you have heard, she was a sad little girl. missing warmth, comfort, a family. despite her situation, the only equivalent to a family was clervie, and that found a horrible ending. if there is something you would like to do, it’d be to hug her young self and never let her go, because even thinking about this story makes you sick once again.

“perhaps you did. perhaps, however, i found myself too occupied with this anthill over there. so, what if you repeated yourself one more time?”

you laughed, your gaze drifting from her mesmerizing eyes to the anthill she was watching diligently just a few minutes ago. she reminded you of a small child watching one of ventis infamous performances. it made you smile.

“what if i told you that i enjoyed taking your virginity because it makes me feel powerful to have taken such a sacred thing from one of the most dangerous people in teyvat?”

she chuckled, dryly, and your hand wandered, your ring finger now joining your linked pinkies.

“then i would think you are a little self-absorbed. retrieving ego from such a thing is a rather manly manner, don’t you think?”

her indirectly calling you a man was not on your bingo card for this peaceful day.

“maybe. but come on, you like that side of me, too.” you tried to save yourself from the embarrassment. for better or worse, she didn’t push afterwards, letting you off the hook too easily. but then again, this break had been going on for far too long. so there had to be a catch to it all, whatever it was.

“i cannot disagree with that. unfortunately.” she grinned. her gaze turned to the setting sun, painfully slow in its wake.

“i have a proposal for you.” she said quietly, her fingers playing with yours slightly. 

“what is it?” you asked, your heart sinking a little. this tone of hers, whatever it was exactly, it sounded threatening, dangerous even. surely she wouldn’t want you to leave her side, to travel back all alone and stay there, right?

“there might be a way for you to see your father again, if you wish to do so. my contacts in the fortress of meropide have located him.”

your heart stopped at that.

“but lyney, lynette and freminet are long gone from there. and you didn’t send them back, so why-”

“that is none of your concern, at least as per usual. however, given our close companionship, i can tell you more, of course.”

in front of everyone, you were but a mere acquaintance of hers. and in front of some selected children, most notably the little team of three, you were her partner. although she never outrightly stated it, but this is how she introduced you. as with many things, you haven’t talked much about that since, but you find yourself to be free of care in that regard.

“after you told me that your father supposedly faced his verdict in the fortress, i couldn’t just sit there and watch, it had been merely a short amount of time since you agreed to help me investigate my origins, so i thought i could be doing you a favour like this. of course, you’re free to decline, if you wish. nothing is planned yet, so it all depends on what you want.”

you stayed silent at that, looking at your hands, trying to pull one finger away. it didn’t seem like the best thing to do - he hadn’t been there for you for a long time, and yet, you missed him sometimes.

“feel free to take your time with the decision.” she added quietly. her ring finger searching for yours, yet, you didn’t meet her.

“you didn’t have to do that.” you muttered, eyes narrowing and sharp teeth biting the inside of your lip. it shed blood not long after; you were rarely gentle with yourself on such occasions.

“i know. but i wanted to. in case you ought to learn more about both you, and me.”

“you wanna make it seem as if you’ve thought about yourself but you didn’t and you know that. you did this for me and there is no shame in admitting that.” you sneered, and she merely opened her mouth. a small part of her teeth was showing, and she closed it again. 

“yes, you’re right. i did this primarily for you. both, as a token of my gratitude, and as a repayment.”

“so the money wasn’t enough?”

“vanessa, you’re doing way more than i would have ever expected from you. you deserve more than just a small pay and a verbal dismissal.” she explained and you took a deep breath. regaining your composure, thinking about your father and how you remembered him - strong, yet a declining mind was unavoidable. that’s when he left you behind; an unfortunate turn of events.

you bit your lip, looking at her.

“thank you. i’ll think about the offer.” you said, half genuinely. and she noticed. it broke your heart to a certain extent, seeing her so flabbergasted, but this was still her own doing. and she was one of the many people who needed to learn how to deal with the consequences.

“you see, i have no desire to reunite with my mother in any way. wherever she is, i’m sure she would be more than happy than to see her ‘king’ has truly taken her place, even if the circumstances of my reign differ much from her initial plans. but, many children, some of which i had the impression of not possessing enough loyalty towards the house, came back running almost straight into my oh so bloody and cold arms. i never expected them to miss me, or any house member that is. but ultimately, they yearned for their family.”

her explanation made your heart stutter, a fast and steady beat in your chest. you hated how easily impressed you were by what she did, the family she built, and you still craved to be a proper part of it one day.

“so you’re saying i’m a kid who needs to reunite with their dad else i’ll be unhappy and unfulfilled and something is missing in my life? i lived long enough without him, i’m fine, thank you very much.”

“i do not say that, and you should know it.” her tone was soft, like she knew she struck a weak spot with what she said, but she also knew you and how you handled such situations by heart as of now. admittedly, you have never yelled at her, nor gotten angry, so whatever this is right now is a first, too. and she handled it with such grace, it made your stomach turn.

taking a deep breath, you surrendered to her seeking finger, your own meeting it promptly.

“i don’t know if want to face him.” you whispered. “i don’t think he can tell us much about our origins.”

our origins. it still felt weird to think about. even if you hail from different eras, different times of the very same land, you both were lost in this world. feeling like you do not entirely belong in it, the nagging doubt and strangeness eating you away at the darkest of times. at least that’s how she felt, and how you found yourself to be like, too. at least sometimes.

“he will be able to tell us something, or at the very least, he will show us.” 

“show us?”

“there are legends, rumors. perhaps even living proof, somewhere out there. people like us, who hail from the ancient khaenri'ah, are cursed. i am, you know that, but that should also expand to you. the curse of immortality, or so i heard. a curious matter, indeed.”

“immortality? what, so you wanna tell me i’m immortal now? i was born and raised, i grew up. wouldn’t immortality conflict with that?” you asked in return, the information you retrieved only during this break already making your mind overheat.

“perhaps. i’m not sure. which is why i arranged this potential meeting with him. think about it.” she said. the premature end of your conversation was sealed with this, as for what to respond to that- you honestly had no idea.

your mind swirled and in response to that, you felt your body weaken as well. the sun had set, and your break had been long extended, so you were sure she would get up and call it a day soon. one of her weaknesses, which had only developed recently. it made you smile every time to think about how this stoic woman became just a little more pathetic for you alone.

“the decision is yours, vanessa.” she spoke softly, her hand shifting, now sliding fully into yours. regretfully so, you immediately embraced her hand with your own, no proper thought behind it. it was impossible to go back to your facade now.

she looked at you and smiled softly. and despite the turmoil and the uncertainty as to what you would do next, you knew you had her by your side.
